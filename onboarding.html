PhotoSync — How-To & Setup Guide

Two-Way Bridge Between Google Photos Shared Albums (Android / Pixel) & Apple iCloud Shared Albums (iPhone)

Pixel (Google Photos) ⇄ Google Shared Album ⇄ Mac PhotoSync + PhotoKit ⇄ iCloud Shared Album ⇄ Partner's iPhone

Fast Path: Automated Interactive Setup Flow

Recommended

Run ./setup.sh (or click "Run Setup Wizard in Terminal…" in the PhotoSync menu bar dropdown). It compiles the native Swift PhotoKit bridge (bin/icloud-bridge) and menu bar app (~/Applications/PhotoSync.app), creates the isolated Python virtualenv at ~/.local/share/photosync/venv, configures your album names/URLs, optionally opens the Google Photos sign-in window, and starts the background sync daemon: 

~/projects/PhotoSync/setup.sh

To change the iCloud Shared Album name, Google Photos Shared Album link, or poll interval interactively at any time:

~/projects/PhotoSync/setup.sh --reconfigure

Step 1 — Enable iCloud Shared Albums on Your Mac

Apple Photos
  1. Open Photos.app on your Mac and press ⌘, (Settings → iCloud).
  2. Check Shared Albums.
  3. Under Shared Albums in the sidebar, create or accept your shared album (default: "The Kingpins") and invite your partner's Apple ID.
  4. Verify that bin/icloud-bridge can see the album and list its items:
~/projects/PhotoSync/bin/icloud-bridge list --album "The Kingpins"

Step 2 — Create Your Google Photos Shared Album

Google Photos
  1. On your Pixel or at photos.google.com, create a shared album (e.g., "The Kingpins").
  2. Open the album's Share settings and enable Link sharing and Collaborate (Let others add their photos).
  3. Save the share link in ~/.config/photosync/config.json (or via setup.sh --reconfigure).
Why a Shared Album Link? In March 2025, Google restricted the OAuth photoslibrary.readonly API scope so third-party OAuth apps can no longer list arbitrary albums. Polling the shared album link directly downloads full-resolution originals (=d for photos, =dv for videos) with EXIF & GPS intact without needing OAuth tokens.

Step 3 — One-Time Google Sign-In (For iCloud → Google Uploads)

One-Time Setup

Google Photos → iCloud works out-of-the-box with zero login. For iCloud → Google Photos (when your partner adds a photo/video in iCloud and it uploads to your Google Photos shared album), sign in once inside the dedicated persistent Chrome profile (~/.local/share/photosync/google-profile):

  • Click "Sign in to Google Photos (Chrome)…" in the PhotoSync menu bar icon, or run:
~/.local/share/photosync/venv/bin/python3 ~/projects/PhotoSync/photosync.py --login-google

Once signed in and you see the "Add photos" button on the album page, close that Chrome window. Background uploads will run headlessly from then on.

Step 4 — Using the macOS Status Bar App (PhotoSync.app)

Menu Bar

~/Applications/PhotoSync.app lives in your macOS menu bar and monitors /tmp/photosync-status.json every 2 seconds:

Menu Bar Icon / Item Meaning / Action
Filled Photo Stack + Green Dot Daemon is running and polling both albums every 30 seconds.
Outline Photo Stack + Red Dot Daemon is stopped. Click Start PhotoSync or Restart PhotoSync Now.
Sync Now (⌘S) Runs an immediate two-way sync check without waiting for the 30-second timer.
Open Google Photos Shared Album (⌘G) Opens the shared album in your default browser.
Open Apple Photos (⌘P) Launches macOS Photos.app so you can view the iCloud Shared Album.
Restart PhotoSync Now (⌘R) Cleanly stops and restarts the background photosync.py daemon.
View Sync Log… (⌘L) Opens ~/Library/Logs/photosync.log in Console / your default editor.

Step 5 — Diagnostics & Useful Commands

Reference

Run a single sync cycle manually in the foreground:

~/.local/share/photosync/venv/bin/python3 ~/projects/PhotoSync/photosync.py --once

Inspect live daemon status and synced item state:

cat /tmp/photosync-status.json
cat ~/.local/share/photosync/state.json
tail -n 30 ~/Library/Logs/photosync.log
